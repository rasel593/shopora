SHOPORA • HELP CENTER

How can we help?

Quick answers to your shopping questions.

How can I place an order?

Choose a product, add it to your cart, and complete checkout with your delivery details.

How can I track my order?

Open the Orders or Order Tracking section in Shopora to check your order status.

How do discount coupons work?

Enter a valid coupon at checkout. Minimum order requirements, expiry dates and discount limits may apply.

How much is the delivery charge?

The current Shopora checkout calculates delivery based on your order subtotal. Check the total before placing your order.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Contact Shopora support with your order ID to ask about cancellation, returns or refunds. Eligibility depends on the applicable policy.

Is my personal information safe?

Shopora uses Firebase services for account and application data. Read the Privacy Policy for more information.

How do I contact support?

Open the Contact Us page in Shopora and follow the available support instructions.
